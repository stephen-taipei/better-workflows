## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and harassment-free
community.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing evidence and trade-offs instead of attacking people;
- giving specific, actionable, and proportionate feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality, and responsible disclosure;
- welcoming contributors regardless of background or experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, or publishing another person's
private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Contact the maintainer
through the private contact method published on the repository owner's GitHub
profile and include:

- the location and date of the incident;
- a factual description;
- relevant links or screenshots;
- any immediate safety or privacy concern.

Maintainers will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest,
and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participation. Knowingly false or retaliatory
reports violate this code as well.

GitHub platform abuse or threats may also be reported directly through
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
