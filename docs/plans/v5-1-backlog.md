## 依賴波次

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  GOV[protected dev] --> W0[baseline and contracts]
  W0 --> A[A diagnostics and recovery]
  W0 --> CD[C intent and D evidence]
  W0 --> B[B admission and isolation]
  A --> QA[combined candidate validation]
  CD --> QA
  B --> QA
  QA --> REL[canary and same-SHA release]
```

RF-01～06 是相容抽取；大型 lifecycle/provider/journal 搬移延後且不阻GA，相關功能安全及四層隔離仍必交。Dijkstra／weighted set cover／ddmin留研究候選，具體缺口成立才開實作子單。
